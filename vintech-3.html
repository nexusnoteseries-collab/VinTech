STORY

Why VinTech

AI changes quickly. A model name, product feature or infrastructure claim can become outdated. VinTech is designed to make the subject easier to follow while preserving a path back to primary sources.


VinTech is independent and is not presented as an official OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta or Groq website.

EDITORIAL PROCESS

How information is prepared

1. Identify the primary source

Official documentation, model cards, research papers, company announcements and official brand pages are preferred.

2. Verify the context

Check the model/version, publication date and whether a statement is a company claim or an independent measurement.

3. Write an original summary

VinTech uses its own wording rather than copying source articles.

4. Preserve the source

Readers can open the source directly from the relevant page.
